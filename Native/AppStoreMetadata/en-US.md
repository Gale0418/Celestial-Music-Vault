## App Review notes

Celestial Music Vault is a local media-library player for Mac and iPad. The free features cover local playback, mounted-NAS sources, search, playlists, favorites, ratings, and everyday queue controls. CMV Pro is an optional one-time non-consumable purchase; it adds offline pinning, smart prefetch, additional themes, on-device acoustic analysis, Smart DJ recommendations, and advanced library actions.

The app does not implement CMV accounts, SMB login, local-network discovery, cloud sync, advertising, third-party tracking, or cloud AI. It does not upload audio, video, artwork, metadata, listening history, or analysis results.

1. Open **Settings → Music Sources → Add Music Source**, or choose **Add Music** in the sidebar.
2. Select a local folder with the system picker. For NAS media, connect the share in Finder or Files first; CMV never requests or stores NAS credentials.
3. Let indexing run in the background, then open Music Library and select a rights-cleared audio item.
4. Test search, favorites, ratings, playlists, queue controls, and background playback.
5. Select a rights-cleared compatible video item to test mixed playback. iPad uses the native player and Picture in Picture; Mac can open video in its separate window.
6. Force-quit and reopen to verify that the authorized source remains available.
7. With an App Review Sandbox account, purchase CMV Pro, use Restore Purchases, pin a rights-cleared item for offline playback, and open Smart DJ to inspect its reasons and play the generated queue.
